What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping research. It may include categories, seller links, prices, measurements or notes. Its contents depend on the publisher; it is not proof of product quality, authenticity or availability.

Is this an official Oopbuy website or a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This is an independent shopping guide with category links to CAFANS. It does not provide an official Oopbuy service, a live inventory feed or a downloadable spreadsheet.

How should I use an Oopbuy clothing spreadsheet?

Choose a clothing category, open the current listing and compare garment measurements with a similar item you own. Check fabric information, included pieces and return conditions before ordering.

What should I check in an Oopbuy shoe spreadsheet?

Check the exact model, size system, seller size chart and any available insole measurements. Review photos of the toe, heel, stitching and outsole. Do not assume that the same size fits identically across models.

Do QC photos prove that a product is authentic?

No. QC photos can help you inspect visible details, measurements and apparent defects. They do not independently establish authenticity, material composition, long-term durability or seller authorization.

How do I compare the total cost of a find?

Add the item price, applicable domestic delivery, service or payment fees, international delivery and any applicable destination taxes or duties. Use current quotes and keep an allowance for costs that have not yet been confirmed.
